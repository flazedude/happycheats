HappyCheats: bundled SMAPI 4.5.2 notices
=====================================

HappyCheats includes the unmodified official SMAPI 4.5.2 installer archive.
SMAPI is a separate program by Pathoschild and the SMAPI contributors.
Project: https://smapi.io/
Release: https://github.com/Pathoschild/SMAPI/releases/tag/4.5.2
Source: https://github.com/Pathoschild/SMAPI/tree/4.5.2

SMAPI, its installer and toolkit, ConsoleCommands, and SaveBackup are licensed
under GNU LGPL version 3. The complete license is SMAPI-LGPL-3.0.txt; the GPL
version 3 text it incorporates is GPL-3.0.txt. These license terms apply to
SMAPI independently of HappyCheats. The upstream copyright, license, and
warranty terms are retained in the accompanying source archive and notices.
No SMAPI binaries or source files have been modified by HappyCheats.

SMAPI-4.5.2-source.zip contains the corresponding source at upstream tag 4.5.2,
including the installer, toolkit, bundled mods, project files, and build
scripts. Build information is in SMAPI-4.5.2/docs/README.md and the build/
directory within that archive. Building requires the documented development
tools and a separately installed copy of Stardew Valley. Game files are not
included. The installer is shipped with its own .NET runtime.

You may modify SMAPI under its license, and reverse engineer the relevant
combined work for debugging such modifications. HappyCheats does not restrict
those rights. SMAPI is installed as separate files in the game's directory,
and the HappyCheats mod uses SMAPI's public interfaces through shared .NET
assemblies. Interface-compatible replacements can be installed with their
own installer or by replacing the SMAPI files while the game is closed.
Retain the corresponding source and these notices when redistributing the
bundled installer or a HappyCheats release that contains it.
