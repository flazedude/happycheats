HappyCheats 0.3.10

- Update Stardew Valley's trainer to 0.1.1. Unlimited use items (F4) now preserves items when giving normal gifts to NPCs, including the last item in a stack.
- Keep normal gift acceptance, friendship effects and daily/weekly gift limits. Special quest hand-ins, machine inputs, shopping, shipping, inventory transfers, fishing bait and tackle remain excluded.
- Preserve existing unlimited direct use, eating, planting and placing behavior. English and Chinese descriptions now explain gift support and its limits.

After updating HappyCheats, close Stardew Valley, select it in the launcher and choose Install / update. Launch the game again and enable Unlimited use items before gifting.
