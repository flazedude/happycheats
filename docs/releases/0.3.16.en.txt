HappyCheats 0.3.16 — initial Crimson Desert Steam trainer

Adds a real native Crimson Desert module and bundled trainer 0.1.0 for Steam build 25477059 / executable 1.0.0.2976, pinned to the inspected executable SHA-256.

Implemented controls: Unlimited health (F1), Unlimited stamina (F2), One-hit kill (F3, amplified outgoing player health damage), Unlimited spirit (F4), and character movement speed (0.25x–3x; 1x restores normal speed). One-hit damage does not bypass scripted invulnerability or boss phase protection. Spirit is the resource requested as heroism.

Teleport to map marker and Unlimited item usage are NOT implemented in this initial release. Their rows are explicitly unavailable and commands are rejected. Marker validity, safe placement/streaming and consumption-only inventory semantics still need investigation. This release does not complete all seven requested controls.

Keep Crimson Desert selected in HappyCheats; losing its matching connection lease for five seconds disables the controls and restores normal movement speed. The DLL remains loaded until the game exits. No proxy loader or game executable replacement is installed. The installer preserves foreign/modified files and supports rollback.

Validation scope: exact executable hash, unique signatures, disassembled player identity/stat layouts and player-only movement chain; offline binary-parser, native behavior, actual MinHook fixture, installer/protocol and existing client/payload regression tests; unsupported-fixture DLL startup rejection; both distribution formats and configured LAN feed verification.
